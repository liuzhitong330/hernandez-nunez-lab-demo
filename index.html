The lab asks how visceral signals, brain activity and behavior work together. Before a neural population becomes a useful readout—or a target for intervention—we need to know which part of its activity is reproducible, which is shared with movement or sensory stimulation, and what an additional experiment would resolve.

I built a spatially linked validation tool using public zebrafish gut and vascular-interoception recordings. It asks a practical question: which candidate cells remain predictable on unseen stimulus trials? The output is a shortlist with its failures exposed, not a claim that a bright cell in a brain map is a causal control point.

How it works

The source study records neural calcium activity during glucose experiments, with UV commands, visual and fictive-swimming measurements. Its multimodal comparison used full-data fits. The public files do not supply a recording-specific map from UV position codes to organs, so this tool tests literal stimulation-position codes—not verified nutrient specificity. Here, models are fitted on discovery data and tested on reserved events. ROIs (source cell segments) are sampled by source index before looking at responses; candidate ranking does not use reserved results.

External data: Chen, James, Ruetten et al. (2026), not Hernandez-Nunez Lab recordings. This is a bounded three-recording pilot, not the full study. Paper · Public data · Reproduce

Research question
Does a position-coded response survive unseen trials and slow-drift adjustment?

Compare the full selected-position + all-UV + motor + available-visual model against one without the selected-position term. Both models include the same slow time trend. Also check whether motor and available visual inputs help; missing visual modulation is not treated as a negative biological result. Positive error reduction is better; negative values are failures to generalize, not results to hide.

What to measure next

Gains are mean per-trial percentage MSE reductions; r is mean per-trial Pearson correlation, both on reserved selected-position events. “Positive” is a descriptive sign check, not significance. Trying many budgets or models after inspecting reserved results makes that inspection exploratory; a new recording is needed for confirmation.

Why this matters for the lab

Whole-body circuit research needs a bridge from association to a discriminating experiment. An ROI retaining position-associated predictive information is a candidate for independent re-recording. A multimodal cell motivates conditions that vary nutrient, movement and visual inputs separately. Neither result establishes control of an organ.

The exported record connects each candidate to acquisition coordinates, source ID, trial support and necessary controls. It can guide subsequent spatial identification, but this dataset supplies no molecular marker identity. Genetic targeting still needs molecular characterization, anatomical confirmation and independent reporter-specificity checks.

What I would bring

My background combines practical assay work and documentation with single-cell analysis and virtual-brain modeling. I want to understand how neural signals become reliable state estimates and eventually inform precise intervention. Here that means keeping the chain from measurement to interpretation to next experiment auditable—and knowing when the evidence is not yet strong enough.

Methods note

Extraction, model definitions, sampling and checks are documented in the reproducibility record.

This reanalyzes author-processed calcium traces from two blood D-glucose and one gut D-glucose recording. It is not raw-image segmentation, a replication of every figure, or a gut-versus-blood comparison. Coordinates are source-acquisition coordinates, not verified cross-fish atlas registration. Motor activity may mediate nutrient responses; conditioning on it does not isolate “pure interoception.” These models describe stimulus-linked predictability, not causal connectivity, a prospective physiological forecast or a stimulation prescription.

The same workflow can accept another recording with time, traces, source coordinates/IDs, verified position-to-organ mapping, targeted/control event times and aligned motor/visual measurements. More animals, unsampled cells, molecular identity and independent perturbations remain necessary before relying on a candidate scientifically.
